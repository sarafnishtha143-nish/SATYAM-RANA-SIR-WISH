08

One Last Thing, Sir...

Written with gratitude — Nishtha Saraf

Dear Sir,

Some teachers teach us a subject, but some teachers become a part of our journey. You are one of those teachers for me.

When I first came to Prime Step Institute, I was completely new to the technical world. I came from a non-tech background and honestly, I didn't even know what coding was, what VS Code was, what Python was, or where to even begin with Data Analytics.

But you never made me feel that I didn't belong here.

You guided me patiently, corrected my mistakes, motivated me when I felt low, and always pushed me to keep learning.

There were times when I genuinely thought that maybe I wouldn't be able to continue with Data Analytics. I had left my business and everything familiar behind in Chhatarpur and started a completely new journey here.

If I hadn't found the right guidance at Prime Step, maybe I would have given up and gone back to my old path.

But having a teacher like you made a difference.

You didn't just teach me Python, SQL, Power BI, or coding.

You taught me something much more important:

"I can learn something even when I know nothing about it today."

Your skills, your websites, your projects, and the way you keep learning yourself inspire me every time.

Whenever I see your work, I don't think, "I can never do this."

Instead, I think:

"There is still so much left for me to learn, build, and achieve in life."

And that thought itself is a huge motivation for me.

I know I still have a long way to go. I still have a lot to learn, a lot of mistakes to make, and a lot of things to improve.

But now I am not afraid of starting from zero.

Because somewhere along this journey, you taught me that being a beginner is not a weakness — it is simply the beginning of becoming better.

So, Sir, thank you.

Thank you for teaching me.
Thank you for guiding me.
Thank you for motivating me.
Thank you for believing in me when I sometimes didn't believe in myself.

I feel truly blessed to have had a teacher like you at such an important point in my life.

Maybe one day, when I look back at where I started and where I reach, I'll remember that there was a teacher who helped me take those first steps.

And I will always be grateful for that.

Happy Teacher's Day, Sir. ❤️

Thank you for being a teacher who didn't just teach me how to code, but helped me believe that I could build my own future.

— Nishtha Saraf ❤️
